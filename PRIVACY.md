# ABattery Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 11, 2026

ABattery is designed to display battery information locally on your Android device.

## Data collection

ABattery does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, account system, or Internet permission.

## Data processed on the device

The app reads battery and charging information made available by Android and, where accessible, device system files. This can include battery level, temperature, voltage, current, charging state, capacity, and cycle count.

Theme preferences, language preferences, and locally estimated cycle progress are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app normally removes this local data.

## Third-party services

If you install ABattery through Google Play, Google may process information under Google's own terms and privacy policy. That processing is separate from ABattery itself.

## Changes

Material changes to this policy will be published in this repository and included with an app update when appropriate.

## Questions

Open a GitHub Discussion for general privacy questions. Do not post sensitive information in a public issue or discussion.
